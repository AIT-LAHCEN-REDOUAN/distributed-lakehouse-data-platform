4. KEY FINDINGS
----------------------------------------
4.1 Customer Churn Statistics:
   - Total customers: 5,630
   - Retained customers: 4,682 (83.2%)
   - Churned customers: 948 (16.8%)

4.2 Top Correlations with Customer Churn:
   Positive correlations (increase churn risk):
     - Complain: 0.250
     - NumberOfDeviceRegistered: 0.108
     - SatisfactionScore: 0.105
     - CityTier: 0.085
     - WarehouseToHome: 0.077

   Negative correlations (decrease churn risk):
     - OrderAmountHikeFromlastYear: -0.010
     - OrderCount: -0.029
     - CashbackAmount: -0.154
     - DaySinceLastOrder: -0.161
     - Tenure: -0.349

4.3 Most Variable Features (Highest Coefficient of Variation):
   1. Complain: CV = 158.4% (Mean: 0.28, Std: 0.45)
   2. CouponUsed: CV = 108.2% (Mean: 1.75, Std: 1.89)
   3. OrderCount: CV = 97.7% (Mean: 3.01, Std: 2.94)
   4. Tenure: CV = 84.0% (Mean: 10.19, Std: 8.56)
   5. DaySinceLastOrder: CV = 80.4% (Mean: 4.54, Std: 3.65)

5. BUSINESS INSIGHTS
----------------------------------------
5.1 Demographic Insights:
   - Single customers have the highest churn rate (26.7%)
   - Married customers have the lowest churn rate (11.5%)
   - Male customers churn slightly more than female customers

5.2 Behavioral Insights:
   - Customers using 'Phone' (not Mobile Phone) have highest churn (22.4%)
   - COD (Cash on Delivery) users have highest churn rate (28.8%)
   - Mobile Phone users have lowest churn rate (12.6%)

5.3 Transactional Insights:
   - Customers with complaints are 2.3x more likely to churn
   - Longer tenure customers are less likely to churn
   - Higher cashback amounts correlate with lower churn rates
