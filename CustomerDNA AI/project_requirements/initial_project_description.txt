CustomerDNA AI - Data Engineering Platform for Customer Analytics and AI/ML-Ready Data Products

Problem Statement: Customer data is frequently distributed across multiple heterogeneous sources, characterized by diverse formats, inconsistent quality, and weak standardization. This fragmentation limits reliable analytics, business decision support, and the future reuse of data for intelligent applications.

Proposed Solution: The proposed project consists of designing and implementing a Data Engineering platform capable of ingesting customer data through a Kafka-based backbone, loading it into a PostgreSQL-based data warehouse, transforming it through layered dbt models, validating it with Great Expectations, orchestrating workflows with Airflow, monitoring platform health with Prometheus and Grafana, and producing trusted analytical and serving-ready datasets for reporting, business intelligence, and future AI/ML-oriented applications.

Main Modules: Multi-source customer data organization - Kafka-based ingestion and raw data loading into the Data Warehouse - Layered transformations with dbt (`staging`, `intermediate`, `analytics`, `serving`) - Data quality validation with Great Expectations - Workflow orchestration with Airflow - Monitoring with Grafana and Prometheus - Production of serving-ready customer data products for segmentation, churn, LTV, personas, and marketing recommendations - Deployment of the platform in a company-owned server environment.

Expected Results: Centralized and reliable customer data management - Automated and monitored data pipelines - Validated and documented warehouse layers - Analytics-ready tables for business intelligence - Serving-ready datasets prepared for future AI/ML applications - A deployable and reusable technical foundation for enterprise customer-data initiatives.

Academic Contribution: This project contributes to the field of Data Engineering through the implementation of an end-to-end customer-data platform integrating ingestion architecture, Data Warehouse modeling, workflow orchestration, data quality assurance, observability, deployment-oriented design, and the preparation of trusted downstream data products for analytics and future intelligent systems.
