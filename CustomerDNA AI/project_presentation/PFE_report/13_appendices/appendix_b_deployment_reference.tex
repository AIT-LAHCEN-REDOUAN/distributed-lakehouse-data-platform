\chapter{Implemented Deployment Reference}
\label{app:deployment-reference}

This appendix summarizes the four Docker Compose definitions supplied with the implemented platform. The objective is to document deployment responsibility and service placement, not to reproduce operational configuration verbatim. Passwords, key material, secret values, host-specific paths, and complete environment-variable contents are therefore intentionally excluded.

\section{Cross-Cutting Deployment Properties}

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering

    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.14}

    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|L{3.4cm}|Y|}
        \hline
        \rowcolor{tableheaderblue}
        \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Property}} & \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Compose-derived implementation}} \\
        \hline
        Network organization & Data-plane services use host networking on the Linux VMs. The local control plane uses its Compose network, while a Caddy gateway publishes the selected Airflow, Prometheus, Grafana, and Kafka UI interfaces. \\
        \hline
        Persistent state & Named volumes retain control-plane PostgreSQL, Prometheus, Grafana, and gateway state. VM definitions mount dedicated runtime directories for Kafka, HDFS, Spark events, Hive PostgreSQL, Trino, Kerberos, and audit data. \\
        \hline
        Identity and protected access & VM definitions mount the Kerberos client configuration and service-specific keytab locations. HDFS mounts TLS configuration, while Caddy gateways protect the Trino and control-plane browser paths. \\
        \hline
        Startup dependencies and health & Compose dependencies establish key startup relationships around PostgreSQL, Kerberos, HDFS, Hive, Spark, Trino, Prometheus, and Airflow. Health checks are declared for critical stateful and entry-point services. \\
        \hline
        Observability & cAdvisor runs on VM1, VM2, and VM3. The local Prometheus and Grafana services consume infrastructure, PostgreSQL, and pipeline-exporter signals. \\
        \hline
        Controlled configuration & Images, versions, addresses, ports, runtime directories, and credentials are parameterized through environment variables. The report retains the architecture and role mapping while excluding secret values. \\
