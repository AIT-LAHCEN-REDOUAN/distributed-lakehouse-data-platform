IV.11 Design Rationale and Trade-offs
The final design reflects deliberate engineering trade-offs rather than maximum-complexity
choices:
• Distributed execution within constrained resources:real multi-node cooperation
is retained within the available VM resource limits.
• Layered responsibilities:explicit service boundaries improve maintainability and
fault isolation.
• Integrated hardening:deployed paths are protected without implying an enterprise-
complete security program.
• Operational transparency:checks, metrics, logs, and execution artifacts keep plat-
form behavior inspectable and diagnosable.
• Future-ready scope:curated outputs support later Customer 360 and AI services
without overstating current functionality.
These trade-offs align the architecture with the implemented workload, available infras-
tructure, and operational requirements.
IV.12 Conclusion
This chapter formalized the stakeholders and requirements of AdOptimizer CDP, mapped
them to the logical and physical architectures, and defined the end-to-end flow and
integrated security baseline. These design choices provide the reference model for the
concrete platform implementation presented in Chapter V.
