Abstract
Modern organizations generate large volumes of customer-related data across heterogeneous
andfragmentedoperationalenvironments.Campaignrecords,behavioraltraces,commercial
transactions, and profile attributes are produced in different formats, at different rates, and
under inconsistent semantic assumptions. When these sources remain isolated, customer
information becomes difficult to validate, reconcile, replay, and transform into dependable
analytical assets. This fragmentation slows decision-making, prevents a unified customer
view, and deprives future intelligent services of a sufficiently reliable data foundation.
This final-year project addresses that problem through the design and implementation of
AdOptimizer CDP, a distributed customer-data lakehouse platform developed within the
broader AdOptimizer AI initiative at SMART AUTOMATION TECHNOLOGIES. The
completed platform combines replayable Kafka ingestion, HDFS bronze storage, Spark
distributed processing, Iceberg and Parquet tables governed through Hive Metastore, and
analytical access through Trino. dbt-spark structures the transformation layers, Great
Expectations records validation evidence, Airflow orchestrates the workflows, Prometheus
and Grafana provide observability, and layered security controls protect service identity,
access, communication, and persisted runtime data.
Deployed across a local control plane and three Linux virtual machines, the platform was
validated through end-to-end execution scenarios covering ingestion, distributed persis-
tence, transformation, cross-engine analytical access, data-quality checks, orchestration,
monitoring, and secure service interaction. The resulting architecture strengthens repro-
ducibility, scalability, traceability, operational reliability, and extensibility. It transforms
heterogeneous raw datasets into governed and reusable data products while establishing
the engineering foundation for trusted analytics, future Customer 360 capabilities, and
subsequent machine-learning developments.
Keywords:Big Data, Data Engineering, Distributed Systems, Data Lakehouse, Customer Data Platform, Data
Governance, Data Quality, Observability, Security, Customer 360.
