VI.4 Data Flow Across Machines
The implemented machine-to-machine flow follows an explicit sequence:
1. Airflow initiates the relevant workflow from the local control plane;
2. local producers read the source datasets and publish records to Kafka;
3. topic partitions are hosted across the brokers on VM1, VM2, and VM3;
4. bronze consumers read the topics and write source-specific.jsonlbatches to HDFS;
5. the NameNode on VM2 coordinates block persistence across the three DataNodes;
