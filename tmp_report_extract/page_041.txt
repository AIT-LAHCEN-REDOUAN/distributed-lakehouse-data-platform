IV.4 Functional Requirements
The functional scope of the platform is centered on the end-to-end lifecycle of customer-
related data, from ingestion to governed analytical access. The most important functional
requirements are summarized in Table IV.2 and Table IV.3.
The requirements were organized as a dependency-aware execution contract rather than
as an independent list of features. FR-1 to FR-5 define the data foundation: source-aware
ingestion, replayable Kafka transport, durable HDFS bronze persistence, distributed Spark
loading, and shared metadata governance. FR-6 to FR-10 cover the controlled use and
operation of that foundation through analytical querying, layered transformation, explicit
quality validation, orchestration, and observability. This grouping reflects the implemented
lifecycle because downstream capabilities are meaningful only after the preceding storage,
processing, and catalog conditions have been satisfied.
