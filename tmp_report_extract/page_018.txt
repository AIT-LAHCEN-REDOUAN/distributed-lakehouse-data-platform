II.1 Introduction
This chapter establishes the theoretical foundation required to explain the architecture of
AdOptimizer CDP rigorously. The implemented platform is not merely a combination of
tools; it reflects architectural choices shaped by established constraints in distributed data
engineering. The discussion therefore begins with general concepts and trade-offs before
moving toward analytical architecture, governance, observability, and security.
The discussion draws on the broader literature on distributed and data-intensive systems,
including the design logic emphasized by Martin Kleppmann inDesigning Data-Intensive
Applications, especially around reliability, scalability, maintainability, partitioning, replica-
tion, and consistency trade-offs [22]. Its purpose is not to survey the entire field, but to
retain the concepts that directly explain the design decisions made in the project.
