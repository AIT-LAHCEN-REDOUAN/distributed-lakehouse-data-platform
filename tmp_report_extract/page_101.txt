Appendix B
Implemented Deployment Reference
This appendix summarizes the four Docker Compose definitions supplied with the im-
plemented platform. The objective is to document deployment responsibility and service
placement, not to reproduce operational configuration verbatim. Passwords, key material,
secret values, host-specific paths, and complete environment-variable contents are therefore
intentionally excluded.
