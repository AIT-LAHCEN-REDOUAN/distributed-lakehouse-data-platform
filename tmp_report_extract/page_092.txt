AdOptimizer CDP Chapter X. General Conclusion and Perspectives
The original problem is therefore answered by a coherent Data Engineering and Big Data
architecture that turns heterogeneous source data into governed and reusable analytical
products. Its contribution is consistent with the principles of distributed data-intensive
and lakehouse systems: durable raw history, separation of responsibilities, shared table
metadata, independent processing and query services, and evidence-driven operation
[22, 11, 25].
The final positioning must nevertheless remain precise. AdOptimizer CDP is a completed
distributed customer-data lakehouse implementation and a Customer 360-ready analytical
foundation. It is not yet a production-scale customer master, a highly available enter-
prise platform, or a finished AI product. Those boundaries do not reduce the delivered
contribution; they define the point from which the platform can evolve responsibly.
