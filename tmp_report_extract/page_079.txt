Transformation and Data-Quality Evidence
Transformation validation confirmed that the raw Iceberg tables could be converted into
the implemented staging, intermediate, and analytics model layers through dbt-spark.
The retained execution traces included model builds and dbt tests, while Trino inspection
confirmed that the resulting governed tables were queryable.
