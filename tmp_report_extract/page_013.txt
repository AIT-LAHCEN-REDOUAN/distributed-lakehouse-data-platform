I.2 Host Organization and Strategic Environment
I.2.1 Company Context
AdOptimizer CDP was assigned and supported by SMART AUTOMATION TECHNOLO-
GIES as the professional framework of this final-year project. The company operates
in a technology-oriented environment centered on digital innovation, automation, and
intelligent systems. Within this context, customer data is treated as a strategic technical
asset that must be ingested, governed, processed, exposed, validated, monitored, and
secured through a coherent engineering platform.
