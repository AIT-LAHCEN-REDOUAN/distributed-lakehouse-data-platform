V.8 dbt-spark Transformation Layer
dbt-spark is used to organize transformations as declarative models rather than as ad
hoc processing scripts. This implementation choice is important because it introduces
structure, dependency control, and documentation logic into the transformation stage [15].
The implemented transformation flow follows a layered modeling philosophy:
•raw lakehouse tables remain the direct output of the Spark loading stage;
•staging models standardize the three source-specific raw structures;
• intermediatemodelspreparereusablebehavioral,commercial,campaign,andCustomer
360 features;
•analytical models expose business-oriented outputs ready for exploration.
