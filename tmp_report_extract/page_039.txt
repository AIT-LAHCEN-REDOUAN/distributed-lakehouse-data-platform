III.5 Conclusion
This technology watch justified the retained AdOptimizer CDP stack against its archi-
tectural constraints. Kafka and HDFS provide replayable ingestion and storage; Spark,
Iceberg, Parquet, and Hive Metastore form the lakehouse; Trino, dbt-spark, and Great
Expectations query, transform, and validate it; and Airflow, observability, Docker, and
the security controls operate and protect it. Together, these choices underpin the system
design formalized in Chapter IV.
