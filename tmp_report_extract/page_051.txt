V.3 Kafka-Based Ingestion Layer
Kafka is used as the transport backbone of the ingestion layer. The implementation does
not load datasets directly into storage or processing services; instead, each source is first
published through a topic-oriented flow that decouples dataset reading from downstream
persistence. This approach improves control, replayability, and operational clarity, which is
consistent with both the Kafka event-log model and broader stream-oriented architectural
reasoning [7, 23].
The Kafka cluster is distributed across the three virtual machines:
•broker 1 on VM1;
•broker 2 on VM2;
•broker 3 on VM3.
