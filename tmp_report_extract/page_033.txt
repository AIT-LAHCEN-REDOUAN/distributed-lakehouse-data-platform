contribution and its future analytical and AI consumers.

design of AdOptimizer CDP. It introduced the evolution of distributed data systems, the
principal engineering trade-offs that shape them, the foundations of Big Data platforms,
the comparative logic of modern analytical architectures, and the principles of governance,
observability, and security in distributed environments.
