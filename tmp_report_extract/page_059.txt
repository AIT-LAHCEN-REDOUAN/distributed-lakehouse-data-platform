V.11 Observability Integration
Operational visibility is integrated from the local control plane. Prometheus collects
service, exporter, container, and pipeline metrics, while Grafana presents infrastructure
and execution dashboards. Kafka UI, the Spark user interface and history server, and
Airflow logs complement these centralized views. Together, they relate workflow status to
service health, node activity, and processing behavior; the resulting validation evidence is
examined in Chapter VIII.
V.12 Conclusion
This chapter demonstrated the implemented engineering chain: Kafka publication, HDFS
bronze persistence, distributed Spark processing, Iceberg tables governed through Hive
Metastore, integrated Great Expectations raw validation, dbt-spark transformations and
tests, Trino access, Airflow orchestration, and Prometheus/Grafana supervision. These
integrated layers form a distributed lakehouse with explicit operational and data-movement
boundaries. Chapter VI examines its physical deployment and operation across the final
multi-node environment.
