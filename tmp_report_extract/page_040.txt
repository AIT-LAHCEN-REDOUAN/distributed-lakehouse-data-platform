IV.1 Introduction
This chapter translates the business framing and technical choices established in the
previous chapters into a structured design baseline for AdOptimizer CDP. It identifies what
the platform was required to do, the constraints imposed by the deployment environment,
the principal actors and consumers, and the way the implemented multi-layer architecture
satisfies those needs. Whereas Chapter III justified the technology choices, this chapter
formalizes the requirements and architectural organization of the completed solution. The
design follows established data-intensive engineering principles: separation of concerns,
explicit data-movement boundaries, and layered coordination between storage, compute,
metadata, and access services [22, 11].
IV.2 Requirement Elicitation Context
The requirements of AdOptimizer CDP were derived from three complementary sources:
• the business objective of creating a distributed customer-data foundation under the
broader AdOptimizer AI vision;
• the engineering need to ingest, store, process, validate, orchestrate, expose, and
monitor heterogeneous customer-related datasets;
• the operational reality of a local control plane connected to three resource-constrained
Linux virtual machines with integrated security hardening requirements.
