VII.10 Conclusion
This chapter demonstrated a layered security baseline comprising Kerberos principals
and keytabs, SPNEGO/HTTPS access to HDFS, TLS-protected browser gateways, au-
thenticated Trino entry, SSH-based Spark submission, separated operational identities,
and optional LUKS-backed storage. These controls harden the distributed deployment
without being presented as a complete enterprise IAM program. Chapter VIII examines
the resulting readiness, pipeline, observability, and security evidence.
