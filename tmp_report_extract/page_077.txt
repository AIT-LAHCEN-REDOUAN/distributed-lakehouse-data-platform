VIII.3 Infrastructure Validation
Infrastructure validation established the operational baseline before pipeline results were
interpreted. The campaign covered Kafka, HDFS, Hive Metastore, Spark, Trino, Kerberos,
the browser-facing gateways, and the monitoring stack [7, 3, 4, 10, 30, 26, 29, 20].
The retained checks confirmed that:
•Kafka brokers were reachable on VM1, VM2, and VM3;
•HDFS exposed an active NameNode and three live DataNodes;
•Hive Metastore was available as the shared metadata authority;
•the Spark master, three workers, Thrift Server, and History Server were reachable;
• the Trino coordinator and its two dedicated workers were available through the
protected entry path;
•Kerberos and the secured HDFS endpoints were operational;
• Prometheus, Grafana, and the configured exporters were reachable from the control
plane.
The HDFS namespace contained two principal branches:/bronze for replayable raw
ingestion and /warehouse for governed analytical storage. The client-specific bronze
branch contained the three retained datasets, while the Iceberg warehouse contained the
raw, staging, intermediate, and analytics namespaces.
