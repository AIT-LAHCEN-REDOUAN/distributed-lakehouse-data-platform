AdOptimizer CDP Chapter I. General Project Context
The broader strategic direction behind the work is AdOptimizer AI, the company’s long-
term vision for an intelligent advertising and marketing ecosystem supporting customer
understanding, advanced analytics, and data-driven optimization. Because such capabilities
require reliable and governed data, SMART AUTOMATION TECHNOLOGIES positioned
the PFE around the distributed customer-data foundation needed by that wider initiative.
I.2.2 Institutional Values and Project Alignment

These values are reflected in the project through the adoption of a modern lakehouse
architecture, the construction of a shared foundation for engineers and analysts, the
emphasis on trusted and reproducible data, the integration of complementary services
into one end-to-end platform, and the operational effort required to stabilize a distributed
implementation.
I.2.3 Position of the Project Within AdOptimizer AI
Within the broader AdOptimizer AI vision, AdOptimizer CDP is thedistributed
customer-data lakehouse platformthat provides the initiative’s Data Engineering
and Big Data foundation. Its role is to prepare governed, reusable, and trustworthy data
products rather than to deliver the final intelligent marketing functions directly.
