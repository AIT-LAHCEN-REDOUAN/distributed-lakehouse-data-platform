Appendix A
Requirements-to-Evidence
Traceability
This appendix connects the requirements defined in Chapter IV with the implemented
platform components and the retained validation material. It is intentionally concise: the
detailed requirement definitions remain in Tables IV.2, IV.3, and IV.4, while the validation
interpretation remains in Chapter VIII.
The traceability analysis is organized around the functional and non-functional requirement
identifiers established in Chapter IV. For each requirement, the appendix identifies the
implemented technical response and its principal verification source. Evidence includes
runtime interfaces, retained validation results, and deployment information extracted from
the implemented service definitions. Each artifact is interpreted only within its observable
scope, while the detailed technical discussion and validation boundaries remain in the
main report.
