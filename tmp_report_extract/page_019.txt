Figure II.1 highlights why centralized designs become fragile under heterogeneous analytical
growth: all responsibilities compete on the same machine boundary, and operational
evolution is constrained by the limits of that boundary.
Distributed data platforms emerged as a response to these limits. Their role is not only to
increase capacity, but also to separate responsibilities such as ingestion, storage, compute,
metadata, and analytical access. This decomposition allows each concern to evolve with
the technical model best suited to it [22].
