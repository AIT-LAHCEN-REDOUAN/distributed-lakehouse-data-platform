IV.7 Logical Layered Architecture
At the logical level, AdOptimizer CDP is organized as a layered customer-data platform
rather than as a monolithic data pipeline. This organization is aligned with lakehouse
thinking, where raw persistence, structured table layers, metadata governance, and analyt-
ical access are coordinated but not collapsed into one opaque runtime block [11]. Each
layer has a precise responsibility in the end-to-end flow:
•Ingestion layer:receives and transports the raw input datasets through Kafka.
•Bronze persistence layer:stores replayable raw artifacts in HDFS.
• Raw lakehouse layer:structures the raw persisted inputs through Spark and Iceberg.
•Metadata layer:centralizes table definitions through Hive Metastore.
• Raw-validation layer:checks the source-specific raw lakehouse tables through Great
Expectations and persists evidence.
• Transformation layer:applies modeled transformations and tests through dbt-spark.
•Analytical access layer:exposes governed raw and curated tables through Trino.
• Cross-cutting orchestration and observability layer:coordinates execution
through Airflow and supervises the platform through Prometheus and Grafana.
