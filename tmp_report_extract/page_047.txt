IV.9 End-to-End Data Flow
The end-to-end operational flow of the platform can be summarized as follows:
1. Airflow initiates the five implemented workflows for environment reset, lakehouse
setup, service readiness, integrated raw ingestion/loading and validation, and dbt
transformation;
2. source datasets are prepared and published through the configured ingestion path;
3. Kafka receives the records and distributes topic partitions across the broker cluster;
4. bronze consumers persist replayable raw artifacts in HDFS through NameN-
ode/DataNode coordination;
5. Spark reads the bronze zone and loads Iceberg raw tables through distributed execu-
tion, with Hive Metastore registering the shared table definitions;
6. within the same Kafka–HDFS–Spark workflow, Great Expectations assets are boot-
strapped and the raw lakehouse tables are validated, producing JSON results and
HTML Data Docs;
7. dbt-spark creates staging, intermediate, and analytics models and applies its declared
transformation tests;
